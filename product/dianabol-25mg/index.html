Chemical Structure

It is a modification of testosterone with a methyl at the C17-alpha position and a further covalent double bond between the C1 and C2 positions. The drug is additionally a boldenone (δ1- testosterone) 17α-methylated derivative and δ1 analogue of methyltestosterone (17α-methyltestosterone). As an androgen analogue, methandienone binds to and activates the androgen receptor (AR) to produce its impact including a sudden increase in glycogenolysis, protein synthesis, and muscle strength over a short period. Metandienone has no progestogenic activity. And as with other 17α-alkylated AAS, methandienone greatly causes toxicity to the liver.

Pharmacokinetics (What does our body do to the drug)

Metandienone is easily absorbed by the body through oral administration. Unlike methyltestosterone, because of the presence of its C1(2) covalent double bond, methandienone doesn’t produce 5α-reduced metabolites. The elimination half-life of methandienone is about 5-6 hours and is eliminated through the urine.

Research in young adult male rats conducted by Hickson, Robert et al. was done separately and in combination to study the effects of anabolic steroid administration on body composition, organ weights, and high-intensity sprint running. Meanwhile, the program of sprint training resulted in lower body weight, a lesser percentage of body fat (Sedentary at 13.5% vs. Runners at 8.5% vs. P<0.01) and a greater relative lean body mass (Sedentary at 86,5% vs. Runners at 91.5% P<0.01).
